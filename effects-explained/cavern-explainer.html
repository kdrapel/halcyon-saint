2. Reveal the triangles

Toggle the edges to compare a surface with its underlying mesh. Rotate the star to see its uneven spikes. The absence of UV coordinates in AURI.ER establishes that this record has no per-vertex UV layer; it does not by itself prove that the object lights the surrounding cavern.

3. From file to view

[Uuve → float32 UV pairs, if present]
triangle count → uint16 vertex-index triples

This viewer reads the first mesh record from each file, scales it to fit, rotates it, projects the vertices and draws triangles from far to near. The two objects are shown separately at different scales to make inspection useful. Their relative size and placement here are not the original scene composition.

What the finished scene adds

Saint’s runtime adds its own transforms, camera motion, rasterization and palette changes. Geometry alone cannot establish the lighting model, the camera path or the cause of a fade. Inspect cavern-frame.mjs for the translated drawing code, and compare the scene around 3:03 in the original player.

This viewer uses simple shading based on face orientation. It is a tool for understanding the assets, not a claim about Saint’s original shading. Triangles crossing the near plane are omitted; close views may expose gaps.
